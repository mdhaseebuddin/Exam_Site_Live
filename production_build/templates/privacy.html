6. Data We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate the platform and deliver the examination service. Specifically, this includes:

  • Account details — your registered email address, display name, and a securely hashed password used for host authentication.
  • Host profile information — the details you provide when creating and using your account, including your saved question bank and the configuration of the exams you generate.
  • Examination & test records — every exam you create, candidate registrations (name, email, phone, and any custom fields chosen by the host), session timestamps, submitted answers, and computed results and scores.
7. How Your Data Is Stored Securely

All platform data is stored in a structured, relational database and is safeguarded with a range of technical and organizational controls, including:

  • Secure authentication — passwords are never stored in plain text; they are saved as salted cryptographic hashes. One-time verification codes are likewise stored only as hashes and are single-use with a short expiry.
  • Hardened sessions — session cookies are protected with HttpOnly and SameSite attributes, and every state-changing request is validated with a CSRF token, reducing the risk of common web-based attacks.
  • Controlled access — host dashboards, exam results, and reports are gated by authentication, and all records are isolated per host, so an account can only ever reach the data that belongs to it.
8. Data Retention & Why We Keep It

We retain your data for as long as your account exists so that we can provide the service. Specifically, your data is kept to:

  • Maintain account persistence — your host profile, saved question bank, and settings remain available every time you sign in.
  • Support session management — keep exam attempts functional, including timers, resumable sessions, and submissions tied to each individual candidate.
  • Preserve exam history — retain past exams, results, and server-side reports so you can review your history across logins at any time.
9. Data Confidentiality & Third Parties

We treat your data as confidential. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we never disclose it to unauthorized third parties. Access is limited to the platform operators who administer the service and, where applicable, the specific exam host who organizes the exam you register for. Where a third-party provider is required to operate a core function (for example, email delivery for verification and password-reset codes), only the minimum information needed is shared, and always under appropriate security and confidentiality terms.
